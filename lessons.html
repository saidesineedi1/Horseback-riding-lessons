LESSON QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about scheduling, riding gear, or lesson logistics? We've got answers.

Are riding helmets and protective gear provided?

Yes! We provide complimentary ASTM/SEI-certified riding helmets for all students. We require long pants (breeches or jeans) and closed-toe boots with a small 1-inch heel.

Can I choose a specific horse for my lesson?

Our head trainer pairs each student with a horse that matches their height, skill level, and temperament goals. Returning students can request their favorite horse subject to availability!

What happens in case of bad weather?

Rain or shine, our state-of-the-art climate-controlled covered indoor arena allows lessons to proceed uninterrupted. Trail rides may be rescheduled if weather poses a safety hazard.

Do you offer discounted multi-lesson packages?

Yes! We offer 5-session and 10-session lesson packages with up to 15% savings, as well as monthly subscription plans for dedicated riders.
